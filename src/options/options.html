Welcome 👋

Open any article and click the Hearline icon (or press Alt+H). Tip: the HQ voices below sound much more natural than your system voices.

HQ voices (offline)

Natural-sounding voices that run entirely on your device — nothing you read is ever sent anywhere. English only (US & UK).

The fast voice recommended

One American female voice in a 9 MB model built for speed: it generates speech faster than most computers can play it, so the reading never stutters.

More voices

28 American and British voices from the larger model: about 95 MB for the six that come with it, and more for all of them. Much slower than the fast voice — on some computers they cannot keep up, and the speed test below decides whether they are used by default.

After installing, open the voice menu in the player to choose a voice. The first six Kokoro voices are included; the others download (~0.5 MB each) when you first pick them, or use “Download all voices”.

No voices on Linux?

Chrome uses the system speech service. On Ubuntu run sudo apt install speech-dispatcher espeak-ng, then restart Chrome. Or install the HQ voices above — they don’t need anything else.

Privacy

Hearline has no account, no analytics, and no servers. Page text is only ever spoken by your browser or by the on-device HQ voices. The only network request is the optional one-time voice download from Hugging Face when you press “Install HQ voices”.
